**Takeaways on price**
- **Cheapest overall:** Rotama, but it is a much shorter trip with almost no meals.
- **Cheapest of the four 7–8 day spring tours:** Obaja, about 6 M below the rest. In return it has the fewest meals, a free day, 4 nights in Anyang (outside central Seoul), and paid extras such as mandatory insurance and tips.
- **Best value per day among the comparable tours:** Panorama. It has 1 more day and 1 more hotel night than Aviatour and Bayu Buana, the most meals, and named city-centre hotels, yet it costs slightly less than Aviatour.
- **Most expensive:** Bayu Buana, even after the 3 M discount. That discount has a limited quota, so confirm it still applies.
- Aviatour's quote leaves out tipping. Ask them for the figure before comparing.

**Notes on flights**
- **Singapore Airlines (4 tours):** full-service, with meals and checked bags on board. All four connect in Singapore, with a layover of about 1 to 2.5 hours each way. **Obaja's outbound connection is tight**: it lands in Singapore at 22:05 and the next flight leaves at 23:05.
- **Open-jaw (Bayu Buana, Obaja, Panorama)** is the most efficient way to see Busan and Seoul, because you never backtrack. **Aviatour** flies in and out of Incheon and covers the gap by KTX train, which costs time on Day 2 after an overnight flight.
- **Bayu Buana has the most relaxed last morning** (11:05 departure). The ICN flights at 08:50 or 09:00 (Aviatour, Obaja, Panorama) mean a very early hotel check-out, so the "grocery stop" on the last day will be rushed.
- **Rotama** has the shortest travel time thanks to direct flights. It is a low-cost airline, though, so check baggage allowance and whether meals are served on board.

## 8. Season check (matters for flower-themed tours)

Bloom dates shift every year. The windows below are typical, not guaranteed.

| | Dates in Korea | What's typically in season |
|---|---|---|
| Obaja / Panorama | 17–23 Mar | **Early spring.** Plum (maehwa) and sansuyu (yellow cornelian cherry) peak around this time, which is exactly what Panorama is built around. Cherry blossom is usually **not** out yet in Seoul and is only starting in Busan. |
| Aviatour | 5–10 Apr | **Cherry blossom season.** Busan's peak may be fading by 5–6 Apr, but Seoul's Yeouido (9 Apr) is typically near peak. |
| Bayu Buana | 7–12 Apr (06 Apr departure) | Jinhae on 8 Apr is towards the **end** of its usual peak (the festival is normally late Mar – early Apr). The 05 Apr departure would be slightly safer for Jinhae. |
| Rotama | 12–15 Nov | Late autumn. Seoul's leaves are usually past peak or late, and it will be cold. |

All brochures warn that blooms depend on the weather and are not guaranteed.

## 9. Bottom line (objective factors only)

| Priority | Best pick |
|---|---|
| Lowest total spend | **Rotama** (short trip) / **Obaja** (full-length trip) |
| Most days and nights for the money | **Panorama** |
| Most meals included | **Panorama** (14), then Aviatour (12) |
| Best hotel location | **Panorama** (Seoul city centre + Jeonju hanok night) |
| Most efficient flight routing | **Bayu Buana / Panorama / Obaja** (open-jaw); **Rotama** (direct) |
| Most relaxed return day | **Bayu Buana** (11:05 departure) |
| Most baggage | **Obaja** (25 kg) |
| Best timing for cherry blossom | **Aviatour** / **Bayu Buana** (early April) |

If cherry blossom is a must, the choice is between **Aviatour** and **Bayu Buana**. Aviatour is about 2 M cheaper and gives 2 more meals and Seoul's Yeouido blossoms. Bayu Buana has Jinhae, better flight routing and a later flight home.
If cherry blossom isn't essential, **Panorama** gives the most for the money among the spring tours.

> Caveats: prices change with exchange rates. Handwritten quotes come from the agents and should be confirmed in writing. Bayu Buana's page 3 (inclusions, exclusions, 07 Apr prices) was unreadable in the scan.
