**Takeaways on price**
- **Cheapest overall:** Rotama, but it is a much shorter trip with almost no meals.
- **Cheapest of the five 7–8 day spring tours:** Obaja, 6–9 M below the rest. In return it has the fewest meals, a free day, 4 nights in Anyang (outside central Seoul), and paid extras such as mandatory insurance and tips.
- **Best value per day among the comparable tours:** Panorama. It has 1 more day and 1 more hotel night than the others, the most meals, and named city-centre hotels, yet it costs slightly less than Aviatour.
- **Most expensive:** Wita (28.96 M) and Bayu Buana (≈ 28.6 M, and only with a 3 M discount that has a limited quota). Wita costs about 0.4 M more than discounted Bayu Buana. For that you get named **4★** hotels, one more meal and better Jinhae timing (see §8).
- Aviatour's quote leaves out tipping. Ask them for the figure before comparing.

**Notes on flights**
- **Singapore Airlines (5 tours):** full-service, with meals and checked bags on board. All five connect in Singapore, with a layover of about 1 to 2.5 hours each way. **Obaja's outbound connection is tight**: it lands in Singapore at 22:05 and the next flight leaves at 23:05.
- **Open-jaw (Bayu Buana, Obaja, Panorama, Wita)** is the most efficient way to see Busan and Seoul, because you never backtrack. Wita takes the KTX bullet train (~2.5 h) between Busan and Seoul, which is faster and more comfortable than a long coach ride. **Aviatour** flies in and out of Incheon and covers the gap by KTX train, which costs time on Day 2 after an overnight flight.
- **Bayu Buana has the most relaxed last morning** (11:05 departure). The ICN flights at 08:50 or 09:00 (Aviatour, Obaja, Panorama, Wita) mean a very early hotel check-out.
- **Rotama** has the shortest travel time thanks to direct flights. It is a low-cost airline, though, so check baggage allowance and whether meals are served on board.

## 8. Season check (matters for flower-themed tours)

Bloom dates shift every year. The windows below are typical, not guaranteed.

| | Dates in Korea | What's typically in season |
|---|---|---|
| Obaja / Panorama | 17–23 Mar | **Early spring.** Plum (maehwa) and sansuyu (yellow cornelian cherry) peak around this time, which is exactly what Panorama is built around. Cherry blossom is usually **not** out yet in Seoul and is only starting in Busan. |
| Wita | 27 Mar – 1 Apr | **Busan / Jinhae cherry blossom season.** Jinhae on 28 Mar sits right in the usual Jinhae festival window (late Mar – early Apr), the **best-timed Jinhae visit** of the three tours that include it. Seoul's blossoms usually come a little later (early April), so Seoul may still be early. The brochure itself predicts blossoms "end March – first April". |
| Aviatour | 5–10 Apr | **Cherry blossom season.** Busan's peak may be fading by 5–6 Apr, but Seoul's Yeouido (9 Apr) is typically near peak. |
| Bayu Buana | 7–12 Apr (06 Apr departure) | Jinhae on 8 Apr is towards the **end** of its usual peak. The 05 Apr departure would be slightly safer for Jinhae. |
| Rotama | 12–15 Nov | Late autumn. Seoul's leaves are usually past peak or late, and it will be cold. |

All brochures warn that blooms depend on the weather and are not guaranteed.

## 9. Bottom line (objective factors only)

| Priority | Best pick |
|---|---|
| Lowest total spend | **Rotama** (short trip) / **Obaja** (full-length trip) |
| Most days and nights for the money | **Panorama** |
| Most meals included | **Panorama** (14), then Aviatour (12), Wita (11) |
| Best hotels | **Wita** (only 4★) / **Panorama** (city centre + Jeonju hanok night) |
| Most efficient flight routing | **Wita / Bayu Buana / Panorama / Obaja** (open-jaw); **Rotama** (direct) |
| Most relaxed return day | **Bayu Buana** (11:05 departure) |
| Most baggage | **Obaja** (25 kg) |
| Best timing for Jinhae / Busan cherry blossom | **Wita** (28 Mar) |
| Best timing for Seoul cherry blossom | **Aviatour** (Yeouido, 9 Apr) |

**If cherry blossom is a must, there are three options:**
- **Wita:** best-timed Jinhae, 4★ hotels, KTX and open-jaw. It is the most expensive (28.96 M).
- **Bayu Buana:** also Jinhae, ≈ 0.4 M cheaper, but only with a limited discount. Hotels are unnamed and the Jinhae timing is later and riskier. It has the best return flight.
- **Aviatour:** ≈ 2 M cheaper than Wita, with Seoul's Yeouido blossoms instead of Jinhae, and 12 meals.

If cherry blossom isn't essential, **Panorama** gives the most for the money among the spring tours.

> Caveats: prices change with exchange rates. Handwritten quotes come from the agents and should be confirmed in writing. Bayu Buana's page 3 (inclusions, exclusions, 07 Apr prices) was unreadable in the scan. Wita's brochure lists the ticket as "CGK-ICN-CGK", but the itinerary flies into Busan; confirm the routing.
