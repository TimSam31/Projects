## Part B — Subjective take (opinion)

> This section is my own judgement. Weigh it against what *you* enjoy: nature vs. city, shopping vs. culture, photos vs. food.

### Aviatour: *the broadest classic itinerary*
- **Strengths:** It is one of the broadest itineraries, alongside Panorama and Wita. You get all of coastal Busan (temple, Sky Capsule, Cheongsapo, Gamcheon) and a full Jeonju day (hanok village in hanbok, shrine, mural village). Seoul gets Yeouido cherry blossom, the Namsan cable car, Hongdae and gimbap making. It is the tour most likely to deliver Seoul cherry blossom.
- **Weaknesses:** Day 2 starts with a KTX transfer after the overnight flight. The N Seoul Tower visit is outside view only. Hanbok is worn in Jeonju, not at Gyeongbok Palace.
- **Feels like:** a well-paced "best of Korea" sampler.

### Bayu Buana: *the cherry-blossom and shopping tour*
- **Strengths:** **Jinhae** is the most iconic cherry blossom spot in Korea, and the tour goes there. Busan includes Oryukdo Skywalk and Jagalchi, and there is Nami Island. Hanbok is worn *at Gyeongbok Palace*, which makes for better photos. It has three hands-on food activities: kimchi, ramyeon at Han River and a shabu-shabu buffet. Seongsu is included.
- **Weaknesses:** It has the heaviest shopping load (Lotte outlet, Red Pine, DFS, ginseng, cosmetics, cookies). There is no Jeonju or traditional village, and no N Seoul Tower. On the 06 Apr departure, Jinhae may be past peak.
- **Feels like:** a romantic spring trip with plenty of retail time.

### Obaja: *the budget essentials*
- **Strengths:** It hits the must-sees (Gamcheon, Haedong Yonggungsa, Nami, Gyeongbok in hanbok, Myeongdong, Dongdaemun) at the lowest price. The free day suits travellers who want to explore on their own.
- **Weaknesses:** It has the thinnest programme, about 8 sights, and Day 5 is mostly shops. It travels in mid-March, so there is no cherry blossom and also no plum or sansuyu stop. The free day starts from Anyang, outside Seoul. A large share of guided time goes to shops, with fines if you skip them.
- **Feels like:** a no-frills sampler, good if price comes first.

### Panorama: *the spring-countryside and culture tour*
- **Strengths:** It is the most distinctive itinerary. Plum and sansuyu villages bring early-spring flowers without the cherry-blossom crowds. You **sleep in a Jeonju hanok**, go strawberry picking, and ride the N Seoul Tower cable car. Samgyetang at a famous restaurant, kimbap making, the Seaweed Museum and Seongsu round it out. It has the fewest forced shops and a free day based in central Seoul.
- **Weaknesses:** **No cherry blossom** (it is timed for March flowers instead). The Busan day is busy, with 5 sights straight off the overnight flight. Ondol floor bedding in Jeonju is not for everyone.
- **Feels like:** a slower, more experiential spring trip that gets out of the cities.

### Wita (Leisure Holiday): *Jinhae at its best, plus a fun day*
- **Strengths:** It has the **best-timed Jinhae visit** (28 Mar) and the **fullest Busan programme**: 7 sights over 2 unhurried days, including Oryukdo Skywalk, Jagalchi, Haedong Yonggungsa, Gamcheon, Haeundae and The Bay 101. Seoul adds sights the others skip: **Gwangjang Market** (great street food), **Eunpyeong Hanok Village** (a quieter hanok area under Bukhansan) and Gwanghwamun Square. Hanbok is worn *at Gyeongbok Palace*. **Lotte World** is a highlight if you like theme parks or are travelling with kids. The KTX ride between Busan and Seoul is a comfortable experience in itself.
- **Weaknesses:** There is no Nami Island, Jeonju or N Seoul Tower. Lotte World takes up most of a day, which is great if you love theme parks and wasted if you don't. Seoul's own cherry blossoms will probably not be out yet (late March). There is no Sky Capsule train.
- **Feels like:** a comfortable, Busan-focused cherry blossom trip with a playful Seoul end.

### Rotama: *the short Seoul city break*
- **Strengths:** It is cheap and short with direct flights. It includes a few sights the others skip, such as Inspire Aurora Square (an immersive LED corridor), Songwol-dong Fairy Tale Village and the National Folk Museum. Seoul coverage is solid for 3 days.
- **Weaknesses:** No Busan and no countryside. November means late autumn and cold weather, with no flowers. It is a different product from the other five.
- **Feels like:** a quick Seoul getaway, not a full Korea tour.

**My subjective pick:**
- **Panorama** for the most memorable, least "standard" spring trip, if cherry blossom isn't essential.
- **Wita** if Jinhae cherry blossom is the main goal and you want comfort: 4★ hotels, KTX and the best timing.
- **Aviatour** for the best all-round sightseeing with Seoul cherry blossom, at a lower price.
- **Bayu Buana** if you want Jinhae plus the most shopping and food activities, and can live with later timing.
